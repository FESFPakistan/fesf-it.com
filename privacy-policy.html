Internal Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

For FESF / Deaf Reach internal systems, online tools, and digital platforms

Official work use only Company-owned records Activity may be logged Requests handled internally

Effective date: May 10, 2026

This policy explains how Family Educational Services Foundation ("FESF", "we", "us", or "the Organization") handles information collected through internal web tools, portals, apps, forms, and other digital systems used by FESF / Deaf Reach teams (collectively, the "Services").

These Services are for official work purposes only. By using them, you acknowledge that your activity, submissions, uploads, and related records may be retained and treated as official work material.

1. Scope and Acceptable Use

Access to the Services is limited to authorized staff, approved partners, and other approved users. The Services must be used only for work-related tasks, internal coordination, reporting, administration, learning, and other authorized organizational purposes.

Important: Personal use, unauthorized sharing, credential misuse, bypassing controls, or using these Services for non-work activity is prohibited and may result in access revocation, disciplinary action, or other corrective steps.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the Service you use, we may collect and retain the following types of information:

  • Account and identity data: Name, organizational email, phone number, department, designation, employee ID, and related profile details.
  • Activity and audit data: Login times, IP address, device details, timestamps, approvals, edits, comments, submissions, and system logs.
  • Content and records: Files, forms, messages, notes, documents, images, attachments, and other materials uploaded or created in the Services.
  • Technical data: Browser type, operating system, app version, session data, error traces, and performance metrics.

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to operate and improve the Services, including to:

  • Provide access, authentication, and account administration.
  • Support internal workflows, approvals, coordination, and reporting.
  • Monitor system performance, reliability, and security.
  • Troubleshoot errors and technical issues.
  • Maintain records for operational, audit, compliance, and management purposes.
  • Communicate service notices, policy updates, and administrative instructions.

4. Ownership of Data and Work Product

All materials created, uploaded, submitted, stored, transmitted, or recorded through the Services are treated as official organizational work product unless we explicitly state otherwise in writing.

The Organization owns the Services, system records, and operational data generated through them to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law and internal policy.

5. Retention, Recording, and Monitoring

The Services may log, record, and retain user actions, submitted content, approvals, and system events as part of official records. This may include screenshots, history trails, change logs, audit records, and related metadata where the system supports such features.

Retention periods are based on operational needs, legal obligations, internal governance, and recordkeeping requirements. Some records may be preserved for longer periods when needed for audit, investigation, continuity, or compliance purposes.

6. Data Requests and Deletion Requests

General data export, portability, or deletion requests for official work records will not be fulfilled as a routine matter, because the data belongs to the Organization and may be required for business, audit, legal, or operational reasons.

Deletion of records may be considered only in special cases and only with written approval from HR and Management on a case-by-case basis. Such requests must be submitted through official communication channels and must be sent from an official organization email address to the HR team and IT Administration.

Note: Approval for deletion is exceptional, not automatic, and may be refused where retention is required for policy, audit, legal, contractual, or operational reasons.

7. Sharing and Third-Party Access

We do not sell data. Access to information may be granted only to authorized internal personnel or approved service providers who need the data to support the Services, security, hosting, backups, maintenance, or related operational functions.

Data may also be disclosed when required by law, court order, regulatory obligation, or to protect the rights, safety, systems, or property of the Organization, its staff, or its users.

8. Security Measures

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the Services and the information they contain. These measures may include access controls, password policies, role-based permissions, logging, backups, monitoring, and encryption where available.

No system is completely secure, but unauthorized access, misuse, or tampering may result in access suspension and further action.

9. User Responsibilities

Users of the Services must:

  • Use the Services only for official work purposes.
  • Keep credentials confidential and secure.
  • Provide accurate information and keep records up to date where required.
  • Follow HR, IT, security, and operational policies.
  • Report suspected misuse, security issues, or data incidents promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Services, operational practices, or internal requirements. If changes are material, we may communicate them through the Services or official organizational channels.

Continued use of the Services after a policy update means you accept the updated version.

11. Contact Information

For policy questions, internal data handling queries, or special deletion requests, contact the appropriate internal team through your official organization email and include both HR and IT Administration where required.

Website: fesf-it.com portal

Reminder: Requests should be made only through official channels.
